Technology That Connects Entrepreneurs With Opportunity

Make relevant government support easier to discover and understand for entrepreneurs who may face information and access barriers.

Our Objective

The landscape of government schemes is vast and highly beneficial, yet many marginalized entrepreneurs miss out simply because they do not know these programs exist, or they struggle to determine which specific program fits their unique profile. Our platform prototype explores a workflow where intelligent matching bridges the gap between available resources and the entrepreneurs who need them most.

Who We Aim To Support

Women Entrepreneurs

Highlighting schemes focused on financial inclusion, training, and collateral-free credit for women-led enterprises.

SC/ST Entrepreneurs

Connecting businesses to procurement policies, margin money subsidies, and capacity-building programs.

Rural Entrepreneurs

Focusing on agro-based industries, rural employment generation, and localized economic development.

Artisans & Craftspeople

Supporting traditional skill sets with toolkits, modernization grants, and market access avenues.

Micro & Small Businesses

Assisting informal or newly formalized units in accessing credit guarantee trusts and technology upgrades.

First-Time Entrepreneurs

Providing pathways to seed funding, mentorship programs, and incubation support for new ideas.
